# Core rules — inhe todna nahi / unbreakable

Full detail: [`../../rules/trading-and-discipline.md`](../../rules/trading-and-discipline.md) and [`../../rules/training-plan.md`](../../rules/training-plan.md).

Ye short copy hai. Agar yahan aur wahan fark ho, lambi file maan lo. **Profit ki koi guarantee nahi.**

1. **Real order** bina Rishi ke clear "haan" / yes ke kabhi nahi. Abhi sirf paper / data collection. Live aur XM EA on hold hain.
2. **Stop loss** ke bina koi setup nahi.
3. **Risk:** A+ (trend, level, volume, news — chaaron theek) par account ka 1%. A (teen theek, news hamesha clear) par 0.5%. B ya shak par 0 — NO TRADE.
4. **Reward** kam se kam risk ka **3 guna**, warna skip.
5. **Lot** = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (Stop distance × per-lot value). XM BTCUSD: 1 lot = 1 BTC. 0.01 par neeche round. 0.01 se kam bane to **skip**.
6. **Daily** nuksaan 3% → us din band. **Weekly** 6% → us hafte band. **3 loss** lagatar → ruko, Rishi ko batao, "haan" ke baad hi dobara.
7. Account **5%** girne par size aadha. **10%** girne par trading band aur report.
8. Haar ke baad **1 ghanta** koi naya trade nahi (no revenge). Price bhaag gaya to **peeche mat bhaago** (no FOMO).
9. Har trade ke saath **thesis** aur **invalidation price**. Wo price lage to turant niklo.
10. Jeet ke baad stop ko entry par le aao, aadha profit Target 1 par lo, baaki Target 2 tak chalne do. Haar ke baad size ghatao, jeet ke baad dheere badhao — ulta kabhi nahi.
11. Badi news (CPI, NFP, FOMC, Fed) ke **30 minute pehle aur baad** naya setup nahi.
12. Data na ho to bolo **"data nahi hai"**. Andaza nahi. "Pakka profit" / "guaranteed" kabhi nahi.
13. **Simons:** naya rule pehle backtest, phir walk-forward, phir paper. Numbers ke bina rule mat jodo. `lessons/` ki strength apne aap koi rule nahi banati.
14. Live / XM demo EA tabhi, jab pass criteria poori hon (positive expectancy, PF ≥ 1.3, DD < 10%, test ≈ training, paper ≈ backtest, 50+ paper trades, 4+ weeks) aur Rishi "haan" kahe.
