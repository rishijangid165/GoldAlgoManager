## What shipped in source

Friday auto-close (server time):

- `InpFridayCloseEnabled` (default true)
- `InpFridayCloseHHMM` (default 0 = auto: 45 minutes before the symbol's last Friday `SymbolInfoSessionTrade` end; fallback 23:15 if the session cannot be read)
- `InpFridayNoEntryMinutes` (default 120)
- `InpFridayCloseManual` (default false; if true, also flattens magic-0 manual positions/orders on this symbol only)

Flattens EA-magic positions, deletes EA pending orders, blocks new entries after the cutoff, and retries early Saturday if Friday flatten was missed. Telegram alert is sent once per Friday when at least one position or pending order is closed.

Trailing after TP1:

- Runs only after the existing half-close at TP1 and the move to breakeven (`ticket == g_tp1_done_ticket`)
- `InpTrailEnabled` (true), swing and ATR both on by default, `InpTrailTF=M15`, `InpTrailATRPeriod=14`, `InpTrailATRMult=1.0`
- Uses the tighter of last closed swing high/low and ATR*k
- Only tightens; respects broker StopsLevel / freeze level; measured against current bid/ask (a profit-side trail may sit beyond the original entry)
- Telegram on each meaningful trail, throttled by `InpTrailTelegramMinSec` (default 300)

Docs (`mql5/docs/SETUP.md`, both READMEs) and the token-free preset `mql5/Presets/GoldAIBridge.set` were updated. Box copies of `C:\MT5\GoldAIBridge.set` (and the Presets/Experts copies) received the same new keys; Telegram token and chat-id lines were left byte-identical.
