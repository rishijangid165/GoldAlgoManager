### Central bank buying

World Gold Council [Q3 2026 Gold Demand Trends](https://www.gold.org/news-and-events/press-releases/central-bank-buying-and-etf-inflows-support-gold-demand-q3) (3 Nov 2026): official-sector net purchases **321 tonnes in Q3** (up 42% y/y, highest quarter since Q4 2024) and **675 t year-to-date** (6% above the same period last year). This is a structural demand support, not a day-to-day price explanation for today’s decline. Q3 total demand was 1,369 t (1% lower y/y) with the gold price averaging US$4,262/oz in the quarter.

### ETF flows

Same WGC Q3 report: physically backed gold ETFs added **211 t in Q3**, with holdings then at 4,256 t. The [WGC October 2026 ETF flows note](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-etfs-holdings-and-flows/2026/11) says global gold ETFs added **US$2.7 bn in October** (19 t), a fourth straight month of inflows, taking holdings to a month-end record **4,275 t**; AUM still fell 6% to US$542 bn because of the lower gold price. Year-to-date through October, ETFs added US$41 bn and 246 t, led by Europe and Asia; North America saw marginal October outflows. Inflows coexisted with a falling price in October — they were not enough to prevent the drawdown visible in `GC=F`.
