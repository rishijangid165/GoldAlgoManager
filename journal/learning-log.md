## 2026-11-14 — Overnight deep study (spot history + walk-forward)

Owner asked for a full-night research pass: gold behaviour from the start of available data, more accurate setup design, and a post-mortem of 13 Nov (why no system trade, where we went wrong). Profit was explicitly not the goal. Details and figures: `research/deep-study-2026-11-14/REPORT.md`. Playbook moved to version 2026-11-14.

- **Data:** HistData spot XAUUSD M1 2009-03-15 to 2026-11-06 (resampled 5m/15m/1h/daily), plus Yahoo GC=F daily from 2000-08 and 1h/15m/5m for the short window and for 13 Nov. HistData 2003–2008 and Dukascopy long history were attempted and not ingested (empty / flaky feed). BLS CPI/PPI calendars returned 403.
- **The important result:** the playbook A-setup is *not* a 15-year edge. Reproduced the original GC=F book exactly (132 trades, +0.37R, PF 1.79). Same window on spot was still positive (+0.27R, PF 1.52). Walk-forward 2012–2026 on spot: A_base **905 trades, −0.03R, PF 0.93, only 6/15 years green**. Split: 2012–2023 **−0.09R**; 2024–2026 **+0.20R**. The recent sample was a favourable regime, not a durable law.
- **Ideas we tested and did not adopt:** half-range stop (OOS −0.05R, more trades, worse book); dropping the 3× ATR cap (scratch); mechanical 38.2% / 50% retrace entries after a close-break (high fill rate, ~10% then reach 1.5R, books negative); London-only as a long-sample upgrade (OOS −0.04R). A D+4H MA gate was the least-bad variant (OOS +0.10R) but almost all of that P&L is also 2024–2026; 2012–2023 was scratch. B-setup including NY-with-trend remains negative on the long OOS.
- **13 Nov post-mortem:** no system A-trade because London **never closed outside** the $48 Asia range (3879–3927 GC). The width filter was not the binding constraint (notes used ATR $14.1 so $48 > 3×$42; our Wilder-14 1h ATR at 03:00 ET was $17.3, making the same stop borderline). The 3909–3913 spot retest did not fill: post-01:00 ET high 3910.2 GC ≈ 3906 spot. A deeper 50% retrace would have been more likely to fill; historically that rejection-retest pattern still does not pay (1.5R after fill only 2–6% on downtrend sells). PPI was a two-sided 3876–3910 spike inside the blackout. Manual mid-range sells were outside the book.
- **Behaviour notes worth keeping:** NY makes most of the day’s range; daily high *and* low often print in IST 18:00–21:00 (NY morning / 08:30 window), which supports the news blackout; Friday has a positive close–open drift (watchlist, not a rule); GC=F–spot basis is a few dollars in quiet months but can jump to tens of dollars around rolls (median +$18 over the 2024–26 overlap).
- **Process:** do not promote a setup from a 28-month window without a walk-forward on a longer sample. Next reviews should ask whether the 2024–2026 edge is still alive, not whether we can loosen filters to trade more often.

## 2026-11-14 11:20 IST — v1.03.1 fix: calendar times 2h early on the live EA
- Symptom (live v1.03 overlay/log 05:38Z): "next Retail Sales m/m @ 2026-11-17T11:30:00Z (block 11:00-13:00Z, calendar)". Real release 08:30 ET = 13:30Z.
- Root cause: v1.03 assumed every `MqlCalendarValue.time` is server time and subtracted `TimeTradeServer()-TimeGMT()` (+2h). Probes show the base is mixed: an unconnected terminal returns everything in UTC (offset 0, so v1.03 looked right in the first self-test by luck); a connected probe returned Retail Sales/FOMC Minutes in server time (15:30 / 21:00 raw) but GDP q/q, Nonfarm Payrolls, Fed Interest Rate Decision and Core CPI n.s.a. already in UTC; the live terminal returned Retail Sales in UTC. Subtracting +2h from UTC records = 2h early.
- Fix: per-record resolution against New York release slots (08:30/10:00/14:00/14:30 ET, DST aware); widen to cover both candidates if ambiguous. Self-test (connected pass): 34 server-base, 6 UTC-base, 0 ambiguous; Retail Sales 17 Nov 13:30Z, FOMC Minutes 18 Nov 19:00Z, NFP 4 Dec 13:30Z, Fed decision 9 Dec 19:00Z all correct (log: logs/GoldNewsSelfTest_v1.03.1.txt).
- Compiled in C:\MT5d: 0 errors, 0 warnings, ex5 221,744 bytes. Not deployed (C:\MT5 untouched). news.json windows were correct all along.
- Lesson: never trust one platform time base; validate each record against a known schedule and test both connected and unconnected states.
- Side effect: the throwaway probe terminal (C:\MT5cal, now deleted) was auto-updated by LiveUpdate to build 6320 despite /skipupdate; the 6320 package sits in the shared AppData liveupdate cache. Keep /skipupdate on live restarts.
