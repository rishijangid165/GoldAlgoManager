## Why NONE (the high bar)

The standing rule is: only publish BUY or SELL if risk:reward to TP1 is at least 1.5 **and** confidence is at least 60, with the entry near a sensible level rather than a chase. Stop would need to sit beyond structure, about 1–1.5 times the 1-hour ATR (19–28 points) or a clear swing.

**SELL** is the direction that matches the tape: breakdown of the 30 Oct low, price far below the 50- and 200-day averages, December hike fully priced, DXY at a fresh recent high, 10-year back above 5%. But a market sell around 3,879 spot / 3,886 futures is chasing a 114–116 dollar down day from only 30 dollars off the low, after four hours of mid-afternoon consolidation. A *limit* sell into a bounce toward 3,892–3,914 futures (roughly 3,885–3,907 spot at a 7-dollar premium) would be more honest, with a stop above the 38.2% retrace and/or the London low (3,929.50). From the cheap side of that zone, a 1.5R TP1 already sits through today’s low, i.e. it needs a fresh breakdown overnight. `valid_until` cannot run through PPI (max about 12 hours → 07:00 UTC), so this would be an Asia-only short into thin liquidity, with Hormuz/Iran headline risk that can spike gold. That does not clear confidence 60.

**BUY** would have to be a retest of the 3,850–3,855 spot / 3,857 futures low, not a chase of the bounce already in hand. A stop about 1–1.5 ATR under that zone can produce 1.5R up toward the 3,892 area. That is a counter-trend fade of a fresh 2026 low into a hawkish dollar-and-yields tape, hours before PPI. 1-hour RSI is oversold, and the 30 Oct washout did eventually bounce, so a rebound is possible — but confidence is below 60.

Therefore **NONE**. The next research pass should look at: (1) a failed bounce into 3,890–3,910 spot with room for a structural stop above about 3,925–3,935, or (2) a failed retest of 3,850–3,855, and should reassess after Asia/London and after the PPI blackout.
